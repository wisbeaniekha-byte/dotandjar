Does the app connect to my bank?

No. You type your balance from your banking app. Dot & Jar never asks for a bank login.

Is there a subscription plan?

No. You pay once and keep it.

Does the app work on iPad?

Yes, in Safari. Add it to your Home Screen and it opens like an app.

What if I stop using the app for weeks?

Nothing breaks. A welcome-back card asks for one balance update, and the jar is right again.

Does the app keep my data private?

Yes. It stays on your device. There is no account and nothing is sent to us.
